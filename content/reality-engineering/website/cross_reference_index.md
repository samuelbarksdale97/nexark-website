# Reality Engineering Cross-Reference Index

The corpus is designed to be read in more than one direction.

A concept may begin in the Constitution, receive a formal definition in the canon, be interpreted through a Federalist Paper, tested in a case, supported or limited by evidence, and challenged by an open question.

This index is the concordance for those movements.
