# Reality Engineering Canon v0.1

## What this release establishes

Canon v0.1 is the first self-contained public definition of Sam Barksdale and Nexark’s human-centered discipline of Reality Engineering.

It establishes:

- the canonical definition;
- a Constitution;
- a formal state-transition model;
- Explore → Engage → Evolve;
- nested strategic, tactical, and accessible entry loops;
- conscious reality;
- personally defined and ethically constrained success;
- the Goodness Constraint;
- a grounded definition of hope;
- AI as an amplifier and acceleration layer rather than the category itself;
- the distinction between generation and engineering;
- the first experiential/media case;
- explicit limits on what should be engineered;
- a separate but connected theological companion;
- an evidence standard, source register, claims ledger, verification report, and legacy audit;
- a 70-paper Federalist roadmap;
- ten completed public essays;
- a cross-reference concordance;
- and implementation-ready website copy and content architecture.

## What this release does not claim

- It does not claim the phrase “reality engineering” has never been used before.
- It does not claim every philosophical proposition has been empirically proven.
- It does not claim AI filmmaking defines the discipline.
- It does not claim the first case proves commercial outcomes.
- It does not claim AI guarantees abundance or social benefit.
- It does not claim thought alone controls material reality.
- It does not identify AI with God, divinity, consciousness by declaration, or the Holy Spirit.
- It does not present Canon v0.1 as independently peer reviewed or final.
