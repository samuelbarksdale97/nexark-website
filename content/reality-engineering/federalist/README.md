# The Federalist Papers of Reality Engineering

## Purpose

The Constitution states what must remain true.

The Foundations paper defines the discipline.

The Method specifies the practice.

The Federalist Papers do something different.

They argue.

They take one question at a time, connect it to the canon, bring in research and cases, address objections, and show what the discipline means in ordinary human language.

The papers may disagree with one another at the level of interpretation. They may surface tensions the canon has not resolved. Their purpose is not to create the appearance of certainty. Their purpose is to make the discipline strong enough to survive scrutiny.

Each paper should:

1. begin from a clear question;
2. identify the relevant canonical commitments;
3. separate founder belief, observation, hypothesis, and research;
4. use current primary sources where factual claims matter;
5. include a real case or clearly labeled illustration;
6. state the strongest reasonable objection;
7. preserve uncertainty;
8. end with a practical implication or open question;
9. link to related papers;
10. update the Claims Ledger when it introduces a material claim.

# Internal Cross-Reference Pattern

Every paper should include:

- **Constitutional references** — the Articles it interprets;
- **Canonical references** — the concepts it relies on;
- **Evidence references** — E-IDs for external factual claims;
- **Case references** — case IDs and maturity levels;
- **Related papers** — supporting, opposing, or extending essays;
- **Open questions** — issues not settled by the paper.

This creates a corpus that can be read in multiple directions. A reader can follow an idea from Constitution to Foundation, from Foundation to essay, from essay to evidence, and from evidence to case.

The links should strengthen comprehension rather than create the appearance of authority through repetition.

# Quality Gate

A Federalist Paper is ready to publish when:

- Sam’s actual belief or question is present;
- the essay adds interpretation rather than repeating the canon;
- claims are classified and sourced;
- legacy statistics have been reverified or removed;
- the strongest objection is stated fairly;
- any theological claim is labeled as theology;
- the writing remains clear to a thoughtful high-school reader without flattening the idea;
- the conclusion offers practice, consequence, or a genuine open question;
- and the paper can survive being read by someone who wants to disprove it.
