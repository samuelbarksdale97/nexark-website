# Reality Engineering Claims Ledger

## Legend

- **B** — Foundational belief
- **O** — Founder observation
- **H** — Hypothesis
- **C** — Case evidence
- **R** — Research-supported claim
- **Q** — Open question

Evidence status:

- **Canonical** — approved definition or commitment
- **Verified** — source checked for the wording used
- **Preliminary** — useful but not sufficient for a broad claim
- **Unverified** — not suitable for public factual use
- **Retired** — removed from public use

# Ledger Maintenance Rules

1. Every new numerical claim receives an evidence ID before publication.
2. Every case claim includes a maturity level.
3. Every founder observation that becomes generalized is converted into a hypothesis.
4. Every correction is dated and linked to affected documents.
5. “Research integrated” is not an evidence status.
6. Unverified claims remain quarantined even when they strengthen the narrative.
