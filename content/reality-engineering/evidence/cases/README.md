# Reality Engineering Casebook

The casebook records what happened when the discipline entered contact with real people, systems, constraints, and time.

A case is not included to make the corpus look proven. It is included to show exactly what has and has not been demonstrated.

## Case maturity

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Concept | A proposed application exists; no working artifact or real-world use has been documented. |
| 1 | Demonstration | A working artifact or prototype exists; outcome evidence is limited. |
| 2 | Documented case | Baseline, intervention, contact, outcome, limitations, and evidence are recorded in one real setting. |
| 3 | Replicated case | Comparable value has been documented in more than one setting or by more than one practitioner. |
| 4 | Evaluated pattern | Evidence supports a bounded general claim about when the intervention works, for whom, and under what constraints. |
